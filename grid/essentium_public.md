## Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti

- id: heritage.funmilayo_ransome_kuti
- role: educator, women's rights leader, anti-colonial organiser
- location: Abeokuta, Nigeria
- status: UNVERIFIED
- boundary: no ownership, no endorsement, no control, no genealogy, not a party

### Work
- Led the Abeokuta Women's Union and the campaign that deposed the Alake over colonial taxation.
  - source: Cheryl Johnson-Odim and Nina Emma Mba, For Women and the Nation: Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti of Nigeria (University of Illinois Press, 1997)
- Mother of Fela, Beko, Olikoye, and Dolupo Ransome-Kuti.
  - source: Cheryl Johnson-Odim, 'Ransome-Kuti, Funmilayo', Oxford Research Encyclopedia of African History (2018), doi:10.1093/acrefore/9780190277734.013.269
- Born Frances Abigail Olufunmilayo Thomas, 25 October 1900, Abeokuta; died 13 April 1978.
  - source: Cheryl Johnson-Odim, 'Ransome-Kuti, Funmilayo', Oxford Research Encyclopedia of African History (2018), doi:10.1093/acrefore/9780190277734.013.269

### Continuity
- work: women's anti-colonial organising and women's suffrage
- status: movement_lineage
- continues as:
  - Abeokuta Women's Union legacy
  - Nigerian women's rights movement
- source: Cheryl Johnson-Odim and Nina Emma Mba, For Women and the Nation: Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti of Nigeria (University of Illinois Press, 1997)

## Fela Anikulapo Kuti

- id: heritage.fela_anikulapo_kuti
- role: Afrobeat bandleader, composer, political critic
- location: Lagos, Nigeria
- status: UNVERIFIED
- boundary: no ownership, no endorsement, no control, no genealogy, not a party

### Work
- Opened the Afrika Shrine in Lagos in the early 1970s as a performance, political, and ritual space.
  - source: Michael E. Veal, Fela: The Life and Times of an African Musical Icon (Temple University Press, 2000)
- Kalakuta Republic was raided and burned by the military in February 1977.
  - source: Michael E. Veal, Fela: The Life and Times of an African Musical Icon (Temple University Press, 2000)
- Died 2 August 1997 in Lagos.
  - source: Michael E. Veal, Fela: The Life and Times of an African Musical Icon (Temple University Press, 2000)
